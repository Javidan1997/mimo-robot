# Upwork positioning

Goal: a small-business buyer reads two lines and knows I solve their problem.

## Who I help
Small businesses, agencies and startups with messy files, slow reports, broken syncs or
slow SQL. Fixed-price jobs of roughly $150-$2,000. They want it working, documented and hands-off.

## Rules
1. **Outcome first.** Lead with what the client gets ("weekly report lands in your inbox
   every Monday"), not the tool ("I use Airflow").
2. **Mirror job-post wording.** Reuse phrases buyers type: "clean and merge messy
   CSV/Excel files", "automate a weekly report", "slow SQL query", "sync Shopify/CRM
   data", "ETL pipeline".
3. **First two lines carry the value.** Upwork hides the rest behind "more".
4. **Scannable.** Short lines, bullets, one idea per line. No walls of text.
5. **Specific deliverables.** Name the files, the schedule, the docs, the handover.
6. **Proof, not adjectives.** Link a repo, a screenshot, or a number printed by a demo
   run (label it synthetic). Never invent clients, metrics or reviews.
7. **First person, plain, short.**
8. **Honest availability.** Project-based, part-time. No relocation or visa talk.

## Never
- Email, phone, WhatsApp, or "contact me elsewhere" (against Upwork ToS).
- Banned words: leverage, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, passionate, synergy,
  world-class, holistic.
- Employer code, schemas, table names or data (GR8 Tech, SOCAR, Azercell, eManat).
- Results that a demo did not actually print.

## Templates
**Title (<=70 chars):** `<Outcome> | <Tool> <Tool> | <Niche>`
**Overview opener:** "I turn <messy input> into <reliable output> for <buyer>."
**Portfolio item:** Title / 2-line outcome / stack / my role / screenshots.
**Proposal snippet (<=60 words):** their problem in their words -> the closest thing
I've built (link) -> first step I'd take -> one question.

## Checklist before shipping copy
- [ ] Value visible in the first 2 lines
- [ ] Buyer words used at least twice
- [ ] Every number is a CV fact or labelled synthetic
- [ ] No banned words, no contact details
- [ ] Under the form limit (verify; title assumed <=70 chars)
